FAQ & recovery playbook

When the league gets messy.

Real leagues hit disputes, no-shows, and mis-clicks. Every answer below is the exact recovery path built into Legacy — nothing here loses history.

While a proposal is pending, the opponent simply reacts 🔁 Counter with a new time. After confirmation, either owner can open a dispute or help case.

  1. Commissioners document the situation in audit
  2. Then: send a reminder, allow a new proposal, award a force win, or fair-sim

Never fabricate a score — force outcomes are recorded separately from scored games.

  1. A commissioner runs /team-release with a clear reason (10+ characters, audited)
  2. The role is removed, unfinished matchups update, and the team returns to Open Teams
  3. A replacement claims it with /registerteam — or is assigned via /importmembers in merge mode

Completed games, career progress, and ownership history are all preserved.

That's the system working. The opponent reacts ⚠️ on the result confirmation — or files a written Report Dispute with a numbered case ID.

Commissioner audit receives the case and the private evidence. The commissioner then approves, requests more evidence, rejects, forces a result, or fair-sims — and only that ruling becomes official.

Legacy rejects a typed score without exactly one supported screenshot in the same message.

  1. React 🏁 Finish again — a fresh 10-minute prompt opens
  2. Type the score in away-home order and attach one PNG, JPG, or WebP

  3. Unauthorized or stale reactions are removed automatically

A team that's already owned, pending, commissioner-closed, or missing from the roster import can't be claimed.

  1. Run /week number:<next week> — do imported fixtures exist?
  2. Fix bot permissions: Manage Channels, View, Send, Embed, React
  3. Run /createweek number:<next week> — it's idempotent

The previous week stays intact whenever opening the new week fails — nothing is lost.
