The rhythm

How a week runs

Owners handle everything with reactions inside their own matchup room. Commissioners only step in to rule.

Schedule

An owner reacts and types a kickoff time. 10-minute guided prompt.

Lock it in

The opponent confirms — or counters with a new time.

Play the game

Adaptive reminders escalate as the weekly deadline approaches.

Submit

Final score in away-home order plus one screenshot of proof.

Verify

Opponent confirms or disputes. Commissioner reviews the evidence.

Go official

One final-scores post, XP awarded, and the next week opens.

Matchup rooms

A private war room for every game

Each fixture gets its own channel where only the two owners (and commissioners) can act. Everything runs on reactions — no commands to memorize mid-season.

  • Schedule, counter, confirm, and submit — straight from the matchup card
  • Force-win, fair-sim, and help requests open private commissioner cases
  • Report Dispute files a written statement with a numbered case ID
  • Every action is mirrored to the commissioner audit channel

Commissioner command center

Every ruling, one dashboard

Open /week and rule the league from a single interactive panel. Decisions are atomic — two commissioners can never finalize the same game twice.

  • Approve submitted scores or send them back for more evidence
  • Force wins and fair simulations, recorded separately from scored games
  • Reopen a final with full XP and standings reversal — after a second confirmation
  • Advance the week: next week opens before the old one is removed
